ICE stops will require body cameras

The Bank of England moved against coal-linked bonds

The Bank of England will stop accepting bonds linked to coal as collateral for key loans. The change gives commercial banks a direct incentive to rethink holdings tied to one of the most polluting fossil fuels. (The Guardian)

Rolls-Royce looked back to narrowbody jets

Poor health carried a GDP price tag
